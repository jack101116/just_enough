The problem

Registrations rarely match who actually turns up, dishes are eaten at different rates, and safety buffers are often guesswork. Too little food leaves guests hungry. Too much wastes money and leaves surplus with nowhere to go.

What Just Enough does

It turns that uncertainty into an adjustable, explainable plan: estimate attendance, size every dish, choose how much shortage risk you accept, see the expected leftover, and prepare a donation plan using a directory of food-distribution NGOs.

{% endmacro %} {% macro steps() %}
  1. 1

    Describe the event

    Guests, registrations, meal, dietary mix and how confident you are in the headcount.

  2. 2

    Set dishes & assumptions

    Portion sizes, consumption rates, no-show rate and optional costs. Every change recalculates.

  3. 3

    Choose your risk

    Slide between shortage and surplus and see the quantity, leftover and cost trade-off.

  4. 4

    Plan the surplus

    Review leftovers, set handoff timing, pick an NGO and track the donation status you record.

{% endmacro %} {% macro sustain() %}

Less waste, more sharing

Food that is cooked but never eaten carries the water, energy and money that went into it. Better quantity planning cuts surplus at the source, and a prepared donation plan gives the surplus that remains a chance to reach people instead of a bin.

All figures in this app are planning estimates. Food-safety timing is guidance, not certified advice.
