The trade-off

Preparing more food makes running out less likely, but raises the amount that may be left over. Preparing less saves food and money, but guests are more likely to find a dish empty.

The risk setting sizes each dish using a documented normal-approximation model. The shortage chance it shows is a model output, not a calibrated probability; check it against your own attendance history.

Safety buffer is kept separate (currently {{ a.safety_buffer_pct|num }}%) so protection is never counted twice.
